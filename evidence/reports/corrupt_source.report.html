7. Limitations

  • Byte-perfect transfer proves the backend file equals the client file; it cannot prove the original recording had no dropped frames.
  • If frames were dropped and the video was then re-encoded with regenerated constant-rate timestamps, the gap evidence is erased; without an external reference (e.g. capture-side frame counter) such drops are not detectable from timestamps.
  • Duplicate detection compares 32x18 grayscale thumbnails; very subtle motion can look identical.
